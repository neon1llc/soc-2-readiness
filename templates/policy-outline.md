---
title: Policy set outline
description: "The headings for each policy a first SOC 2 report needs. Fill in what your company does."
---

Every policy opens with the same header: **Owner** (name, role) · **Version** · **Approved by** (name, date) · **Next review** (date). Keep each to one to three pages in plain "we" and "you," stating rules, not tools. See [module 5](../course/05-policies.md).

| Policy | Headings |
|---|---|
| Information security | Purpose and scope · Roles · How the other policies fit together · Exceptions · Consequences |
| Acceptable use | Company devices and accounts · Personal devices · Data you may and may not share · Reporting a lost device or suspicious message · Monitoring notice |
| Access control | Least privilege · Requesting and approving access · Two-step sign-in and single sign-on · Administrator and production access · Quarterly reviews · Removal within one business day of departure · Service accounts and secrets |
| Change management | What counts as a change · Review by someone other than the author · Testing and checks · Deploying through the pipeline · Separate environments and test data · Emergency changes |
| Incident response | What an incident is · How to report one · Severity levels · Roles · Detect, contain, fix, recover, review · Customer and legal notice · Evidence handling · Yearly tabletop |
| Business continuity and backup | Critical systems and recovery targets · Backup frequency, retention, encryption, and separation · Yearly restore test · Loss of a key system, provider, or person · Yearly review |
| Vendor management | Risk tiers · Review before signing · Yearly reviews for high-risk vendors · Contract terms · Complementary controls from vendor reports · Offboarding a vendor |
| Data classification and retention | Classes (public, internal, confidential, customer) · Handling rules · Retention periods · Deletion on request and at contract end · Secure disposal |
| Risk management | Method and scoring scale · Yearly and on-change assessments · Treatments and acceptance · Owners |
| Human resources security | Background checks · Confidentiality agreements · Training at hire and yearly · Disciplinary process · Offboarding checklist |
| Encryption and key management | Data in transit · Data at rest · Key storage, access, and rotation · Approved algorithms and protocols, named in a procedure |
