---
title: System description outline
description: "Headings for drafting the system description that opens a SOC 2 report, and the scope decisions behind it."
---

Draft this in [module 2](../course/02-scope.md) and refine it before the audit. The auditor helps shape the wording, but the facts are yours.

1. **The company and the service.** Who you are, what the service does, and who uses it.
2. **Principal service commitments and system requirements.** What contracts, SLAs, and your trust page promise about security (and availability or confidentiality, if in scope), and the requirements that make those promises true.
3. **Components.** Infrastructure (providers, regions, accounts, networks, with a diagram), software, people (teams and headcount), procedures (access, change, operations, incidents), and data (what, where, classification).
4. **Boundaries.** What's in scope, what's out, and why.
5. **Criteria in scope.** Security, plus any others, with a reason for each.
6. **Subservice organizations.** Each provider, what it does, whether it's carved out, and the controls you rely on it for.
7. **Complementary user entity controls.** What customers must do for your controls to work, such as managing their own users.
8. **Significant changes during the period.** A new system, provider, reorganization, or anything else major.
9. **Incidents** that affected your commitments.
