---
title: "Evidence: incidents"
description: "Proof that an incident plan exists, incidents are handled and reviewed, and the plan is exercised each year."
---

Controls: INC-01 to INC-03.

## Auditor asks for

- [ ] The approved incident response plan: roles, severity levels, contact steps, and customer notice rules.
- [ ] The incident log for the window (the population), even if it's short.
- [ ] For each incident or a sample: timeline, actions, communication, closure, and lessons learned. The [incident report template](../templates/incident-report.md) covers these.
- [ ] The yearly tabletop: scenario, attendees, notes, and follow-up tickets.
- [ ] Evidence staff know how to report a security concern, such as onboarding material or a posted address.

## Good looks like

Include low-severity events (a phishing email reported, a lost phone wiped). An empty log for a year makes auditors wonder whether events are recorded.

## Common exceptions

- An incident handled in chat with no ticket, so it's missing from the population.
- No lessons learned after a significant incident.
- The tabletop skipped, or held without notes.
