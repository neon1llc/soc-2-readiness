## Why the reason matters

SOC 2 takes sustained time from at least one person, plus an audit firm's fee. Fees vary, so get quotes first. Without a clear reason, scope creeps and the work stalls. Usually a customer asked, and that sets your date and often your scope, so ask what they need covered.

## When SOC 2 isn't the answer yet

A lighter step can close the deal for now: a completed security questionnaire (the customer's, or a standard one such as the CAIQ or SIG Lite) with a short security overview page, or a letter naming your readiness work and the date you expect the report. If most customers are outside the United States, consider ISO 27001 instead. The [controls map](../controls/controls-map.md) shows the overlap.

## Doing it with help

- **A compliance platform** (Vanta, Drata, and Secureframe are examples, not endorsements) collects evidence from your cloud, identity, and ticketing tools. It doesn't set your scope or run your reviews, and it costs a subscription.
- **Your auditor's readiness assessment** shows whether your design looks right to the firm that may issue the report. Use it before a Type II window.
- **A consultant or virtual CISO** helps if nobody on the team has done this before.

## Type I or Type II

| | Type I | Type II |
|---|---|---|
| Says | Controls were designed well on one date | Controls also *worked* over an observation window of three to twelve months |
| Takes | Weeks after readiness | The window plus a few weeks |

Most customers ask for a Type II. A common path is a Type I, then a Type II with a three-month window, then twelve-month windows. If your customer accepts only a Type II, skip the Type I. Ask them which type and criteria they need, and by when.

## Exercise

On one page, write who asked, for what, and by when. Add what happens without a report by then, Type I or Type II, the date the window must start, and who owns the work and what they'll stop doing. Get leadership's yes on the page. It is your first evidence for control [GOV-01](../controls/controls-map.md#governance).
