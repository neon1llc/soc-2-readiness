title: The course
description: "Eight modules that take a small or growing company from a customer's first SOC 2 question to being prepared for a first audit."
---

Each module takes about an hour: twenty minutes to read and forty for the exercise on your company. Do one a week, in order, alongside the [90-day plan](../plan/90-day-plan.md). Unfamiliar words are in the [glossary](glossary.md).

| # | Module | You leave with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Decide why, and which report](01-decide-why.md) | A reason, a target date, and Type I or Type II |
| 2 | [Draw the boundary](02-scope.md) | A scope statement and a vendor list |
| 3 | [Find your real risks](03-risk.md) | A risk register with owners |
| 4 | [Write controls you can keep](04-controls.md) | Your controls list, cut down from the map |
| 5 | [Policies people read](05-policies.md) | A policy set with owners and an approval date |
| 6 | [Evidence that holds up](06-evidence.md) | An evidence tracker and your first proof |
| 7 | [Run it all year](07-operate.md) | A calendar of recurring controls and owners |
| 8 | [The audit, and after](08-the-audit.md) | An auditor shortlist and a plan for the report |

Before you start, decide who owns this work, by name, with how many hours a week, and which customer, deal, or date is driving it.
