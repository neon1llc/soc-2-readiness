**Audience:** founders, operators, security owners, and engineering leads preparing for a first report, in groups of 4 to 20 with two or three people per company. **Bring:** a projector, a laptop per team, the printed [controls map](../controls/controls-map.md) and [evidence checklists](../evidence/index.md), and the [templates](../templates/index.md) shared ahead.

## Half-day format (4 hours)

| Time | Segment | Material |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Each team says who asked for SOC 2 and by when | [Module 1](../course/01-decide-why.md) |
| 0:15 | Type I or Type II, and when something lighter will do | Module 1 |
| 0:35 | Draw your system on one page | [Module 2](../course/02-scope.md) exercise |
| 1:05 | Fifteen risks in fifteen minutes, then score the top five | [Module 3](../course/03-risk.md) exercise |
| 1:40 | Break | |
| 1:50 | Rewrite three weak controls together | [Module 4](../course/04-controls.md) |
| 2:20 | Each team marks keep, rewrite, start, or cut for one area | Controls map |
| 2:50 | Weak and strong evidence, and how sampling works | [Module 6](../course/06-evidence.md) |
| 3:20 | Calendar, auditor questions, the report | [Modules 7](../course/07-operate.md) and [8](../course/08-the-audit.md) |
| 3:45 | Each team names its owner, date, and first three tasks | [90-day plan](../plan/90-day-plan.md) |

## Two-day format

Day one is modules 1 to 4 with the full exercises, ending with each team's controls list. Day two is modules 5 to 8: teams draft an access control policy, fill the evidence tracker for one area, run a 30-minute tabletop, and role-play an auditor walkthrough of one control.

## Exercises that work in a room

- **Spot the weak control.** Show five control statements, some vague. Teams fix the vague ones and name the four parts.
- **Evidence sorting.** Teams sort printed examples (a cropped screenshot, a dated export, a chat message, a signed ticket) into would-pass and would-fail, and say why.
- **Auditor role-play.** One person asks for the population and three samples for one control. The team says where each would come from.
- **Tabletop in miniature.** "A customer emails to say their data is on a public forum." Ten minutes: who's called, who decides on customer notice, where the logs are.

*Can we do it without a security person?* Yes, with a named owner who has real time. An operations or engineering lead can run a first report.

Afterward, send each team the course links, their marked-up controls list, and the date they named. Check in at 30 days.
